# Rotatables

Rotating A and rotating B are neighbors.

Left or right arrow key selects one of them.

Pressing spacebar toggles a different skin (from perhaps 5 options). A skin can showcase:

- special effects for the fill, say spotlights sweeping across the surface, or cracks forming and healing randomly
- special effects at the edges, say tiny lightning streaks shooting out, or vapor from a freezing surface

The game should be a copy of Barebones, but refactored to have the final product.

Prefer smaller, focused files and functions.
